The policy language has three syntaxes that describe the same policy. This is sample copy: it exists
to give the layout real structure, not to document anything.

- **Blocks** names every part of the policy as a block.
- **Rules** writes each fact as one line.
- **Actors** groups the policy by who acts.

The panel beside this text shows the same complete policy in each syntax. Pick the one your team reads fastest.
